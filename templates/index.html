Taste of Ireland

Good food,
good company.

Irish food is warm, generous, and made for lingering at the table. Start with something simple and stay for one more cup.

01

Soda bread

Golden-crusted, hearty, and best served warm with butter.

02

Irish stew

A comforting bowl of slow-cooked meat, potatoes, and rich broth.

03

Shepherd's pie

A hearty baked dish of savory meat, vegetables, and creamy mashed potatoes.

04

Colcannon

Creamy mashed potatoes mixed with cabbage, kale, and plenty of butter.

05

Boxty

A traditional Irish potato pancake, crisp at the edges and soft inside.

06

Apple tart

A warm, homely dessert made with Irish apples and served with cream.

07

Irish coffee

Hot coffee blended with Irish whiskey, sugar, and a soft layer of lightly whipped cream.

08

Irish Cream

A smooth, creamy Irish liqueur with rich notes of coffee, cocoa, and whiskey.

09

Hot Irish Whiskey

A warming blend of Irish whiskey, hot water, sugar, and a bright slice of lemon.

10

Irish cider

Fresh, crisp, and lightly sparkling, made from locally grown apples.

A little more to know

Ireland
in brief.

01

Capital city

Dublin is Ireland's capital and largest city, set along the east coast.

02

Two languages

Irish and English are the official languages of the Republic of Ireland.

03

Currency

The euro is the currency used in the Republic of Ireland.

04

National day

St. Patrick's Day is celebrated each year on March 17.

05

Ancient passage tomb

Newgrange was built around 3200 BCE, making it older than Stonehenge and the Egyptian pyramids.

06

Halloween beginnings

Halloween has roots in Samhain, an ancient Gaelic festival marking the start of winter.

07

National symbol

The harp is Ireland's official national symbol and appears on government emblems and coins.

08

Atlantic adventure

The Wild Atlantic Way follows roughly 2,500 kilometres of Ireland's dramatic western coastline.

09

Musical tradition

Traditional Irish music often features the fiddle, tin whistle, bodhran, and uilleann pipes.

10

Gaelic games

Hurling is one of the world's oldest field sports and is deeply woven into Irish community life.

11

Literary giants

Ireland has produced four Nobel Prize winners in literature, including W. B. Yeats and Seamus Heaney.

12

Fairy forts

Ancient ringforts are linked to Irish folklore and are traditionally treated with great respect.

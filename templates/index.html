Taste of Ireland

Good food,
good company.

Irish food is warm, generous, and made for lingering at the table. Start with something simple and stay for one more cup.

01

Soda bread

Golden-crusted, hearty, and best served warm with butter.

02

Irish stew

A comforting bowl of slow-cooked meat, potatoes, and rich broth.

03

Fresh seafood

Oysters, salmon, and mussels gathered from Ireland's Atlantic coast.

04

Colcannon

Creamy mashed potatoes mixed with cabbage, kale, and plenty of butter.

05

Boxty

A traditional Irish potato pancake, crisp at the edges and soft inside.

06

Apple tart

A warm, homely dessert made with Irish apples and served with cream.

07

Irish tea

Strong black tea, usually served with milk, conversation, and something sweet.

08

Irish stout

Dark, smooth, and rich, with a creamy pour made for slow evenings.

09

Irish whiskey

A smooth, golden spirit enjoyed neat, over ice, or in a classic drink.

10

Irish cider

Fresh, crisp, and lightly sparkling, made from locally grown apples.
